Phase 1 — Question Selection and Tourism Mapping
The WVS questionnaire was not treated as a tourism survey. Questions were first audited for behavioral relevance. A question was retained only when its meaning could reasonably support a tourism-related behavioral construct. Politically specific, sensitive, or unsupported relationships were not used as Hobby evidence.
313 WVS questions were reviewed.
50 questions were retained as tourism-relevant.
37 unique questions were used in direct Question × Hobby mappings.
Those 37 questions produced 101 Question × Hobby relationships because one question can support more than one Hobby.

Scoring Method
The scoring keeps two concepts separate: Question-to-Hobby relevance and respondent answer evidence.

How to Read the Environment Results
Each row in the Environment results represents one Birth Country × Environment result. Birth Country is derived from Q266, while ISO3 provides the standardized country code used for consistent identification across outputs. Environment identifies whether the derived signal relates to Indoor, Outdoor, or Both activities. WVS-Derived Signal (%) is the final weighted environment score for that Birth-Country group, while Global Baseline (%) is the overall weighted WVS reference score for the same environment. Relative vs Global (pp) is calculated as the country signal minus the global baseline, showing how far the group is above or below the overall WVS reference in percentage points. Raw N, Effective N, and Question Coverage (%) describe the amount and quality of evidence supporting the result. Eligible for Ranking indicates whether the row meets the project's defined quality requirements for comparison, and Country Rank shows its position among eligible Birth-Country groups for the same environment.

Important Limitations
Limitation
What it means
Q266
Country of Birth, not confirmed legal nationality.
WVS
A behavioral/value survey, not a direct tourism-preference survey.
Hobby signal
An inferred behavioral score, not a literal population percentage.
Semantic weights
Transparent and auditable, but not yet calibrated against real Survey ground truth.
Individual variation
People within the same Birth-Country group can differ substantially.
Next Step
